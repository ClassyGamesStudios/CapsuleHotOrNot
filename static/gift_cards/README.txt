Gift-card art for the player join carousel (/player/join).

Drop image files here and they join the carousel automatically — no code change.
Shown in filename order, which is why these are numbered (01_, 02_, ...).

The caption under each card comes from the filename:
    01_steam-wallet-10.svg  ->  "Steam Wallet $10"
(index prefix stripped, dashes/underscores become spaces, a trailing number
becomes a dollar amount).

Accepted: .png .jpg .jpeg .gif .webp .svg   Best size: 320x192-ish, card shaped.

The .svg files here are PLACEHOLDERS — replace them with real card artwork.
